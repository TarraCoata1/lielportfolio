Velvet Snow

Soft, creamy and pampering. Wrapped feminine winter.

₪ 350

Scent Profile

Top Notes

Pear, Bergamot

Heart Notes

Turkish Rose, White Orchid

Base Notes

Vanilla, Soft Musk, Creamy Sandalwood

Velvet Snow is a soft winter reverie. Crisp pear and bergamot open the fragrance with a sparkling coolness. At its heart lies a bouquet of lush Turkish rose and rare white orchid, offering a creamy floral warmth. The base notes – smooth vanilla, soft musk, and creamy sandalwood – feel like cashmere at dusk. Gentle and enveloping, this scent wraps the wearer in a feminine embrace.
