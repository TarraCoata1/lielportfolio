Fragrance Tips

Storage

Store perfumes away from light and heat for longevity.

Application

Apply to pulse points for the best projection.

Layering

Layer scents by using matching body products.

Development

Let the fragrance develop on your skin for 30 minutes before judging.

About Divina Maison

Divina Maison was born from a passion for the art of perfumery and the belief that every individual deserves a signature scent that reflects their unique essence. Our journey began with a simple vision: to create luxury fragrances that are both accessible and extraordinary.

Each perfume is handcrafted in small batches by our master perfumers, using rare ingredients sourced from around the world. We believe in the power of scent to evoke emotion, create memories, and express identity. Our commitment to quality and craftsmanship ensures that every bottle tells a story.
